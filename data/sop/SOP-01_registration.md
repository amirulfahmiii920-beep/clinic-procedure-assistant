# SOP-01: Patient Registration

> Fictional clinic for a training project. Klinik Kesihatan Taman Mawar does not exist.
> Document version 1.2, effective 1 July 2026.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure explains how patients register at the counter before screening.

## 2. Documents to bring
2.1 Malaysian adults must bring their MyKad (identity card).
2.2 Children under 12 must bring their MyKid or birth certificate. The parent or guardian must bring their own MyKad.
2.3 Patients with an appointment must also bring their appointment card or the appointment SMS.
2.4 Non-citizens must bring their passport or a valid work permit.
2.5 Patients on long-term medication should bring their medication record book.

## 3. Forgotten identity card
3.1 A Malaysian patient who forgot their MyKad may still register if the counter can find their record by full name and date of birth.
3.2 If no record can be found, the patient is registered as a new patient and must show the MyKad at the next visit.
3.3 Emergency cases are never turned away for missing documents.

## 4. Registration steps
4.1 Take a number from the registration kiosk at the entrance.
4.2 Wait until the number is called at Counter 1 or Counter 2.
4.3 Hand over the documents listed in Section 2.
4.4 The counter staff confirm the reason for the visit and print a screening slip.
4.5 Proceed to the screening area with the screening slip.

## 5. Registration hours
Registration closes 30 minutes before the clinic closes. Patients who arrive after registration closes are asked to return the next working day, unless the case is an emergency.
