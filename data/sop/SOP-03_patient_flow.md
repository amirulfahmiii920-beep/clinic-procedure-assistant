# SOP-03: Patient Flow

> Fictional clinic for a training project. Klinik Kesihatan Taman Mawar does not exist.
> Document version 2.0, effective 1 March 2026.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure explains the steps a patient moves through from arrival to collecting medicine.

## 2. The two queues
2.1 The clinic runs two separate queues: the screening queue and the doctor queue.
2.2 A patient joins the doctor queue only after screening is complete.
2.3 The waiting time shown for the doctor queue starts counting from the end of screening, not from registration.

## 3. Steps
3.1 Registration at Counter 1 or Counter 2 (see SOP-01).
3.2 Screening at the screening area: a nurse takes blood pressure, temperature, weight and a short history.
3.3 Waiting area B: wait for the doctor queue number to appear on the screen.
3.4 Consultation with the doctor in the room shown on the screen.
3.5 Pharmacy: hand the prescription slip to the pharmacy counter and wait for your number.

## 4. Priority patients
4.1 Elderly patients aged 60 and above, pregnant women, and patients with disabilities are called first within each queue.
4.2 Patients whose screening shows urgent signs are sent directly to the treatment room and do not wait in the doctor queue.

## 5. Missed number
If a patient misses their number, they should return to the screening area counter. The number is placed back into the queue after the next three patients.
