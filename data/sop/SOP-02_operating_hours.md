# SOP-02: Operating Hours

> Fictional clinic for a training project. Klinik Kesihatan Taman Mawar does not exist.
> Document version 1.0, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure sets the opening hours of the outpatient clinic.

## 2. Weekday hours
2.1 Monday to Thursday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.
2.2 Friday: the clinic opens at 8:00 am and closes at 5:00 pm, with a Friday prayer break from 12:15 pm to 2:45 pm.
2.3 Lunch break, Monday to Thursday: 1:00 pm to 2:00 pm. Registration pauses during the break, but patients already registered continue to be seen.

## 3. Weekends and public holidays
3.1 The outpatient clinic is closed on Saturday, Sunday and public holidays.
3.2 On those days, patients should go to the Emergency Department of Hospital Daerah Mawar.

## 4. Pharmacy hours
The pharmacy follows the clinic hours and closes 15 minutes after the last doctor finishes, so patients can still collect medicine after their consultation.

## 5. Registration cut-off
Registration closes 30 minutes before the clinic closes (4:30 pm on Monday to Friday). See SOP-01 Section 5.
