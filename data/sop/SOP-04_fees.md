# SOP-04: Fees and Exemptions

> Fictional clinic for a training project. Klinik Kesihatan Taman Mawar does not exist.
> Fees below are invented for this exercise and are not real government charges.
> Document version 1.1, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure lists the outpatient fees and who is exempt from them.

## 2. Outpatient fees
2.1 Malaysian citizens: RM1.00 per visit, including consultation and basic medicine.
2.2 Non-citizens: RM40.00 per visit for consultation. Medicine is charged separately.
2.3 Fees are paid at the registration counter before screening.

## 3. Exemptions for citizens
3.1 Senior citizens aged 60 and above pay no fee.
3.2 Children under 12 pay no fee.
3.3 Registered persons with disabilities (OKU card holders) pay no fee.
3.4 Government pensioners pay no fee when they show their pensioner card.

## 4. Payment methods
4.1 Cash and debit card are accepted at the registration counter.
4.2 E-wallet payments are accepted for amounts of RM10.00 and above.

## 5. Receipts
A receipt is printed for every payment. Lost receipts can be reprinted at the counter on the same day only.
