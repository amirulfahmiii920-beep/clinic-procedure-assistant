# SOP-05: Appointments and Special Cases

> Fictional clinic for a training project. Klinik Kesihatan Taman Mawar does not exist.
> Document version 1.3, effective 1 May 2026.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure covers follow-up appointments, late arrivals and emergencies.

## 2. Changing an appointment
2.1 Patients may change an appointment by calling the clinic at least one working day before the appointment date.
2.2 Patients may also change the appointment in person at the registration counter.
2.3 An appointment may be changed a maximum of two times. After that, the patient must register as a walk-in.

## 3. Late arrival
3.1 Patients who arrive up to 30 minutes after their appointment time are still seen, but after patients who arrived on time.
3.2 Patients who arrive more than 30 minutes late are registered as walk-in patients for that day.

## 4. Missed appointments
A patient who misses an appointment without notice must call the clinic or come to the counter to get a new appointment date.

## 5. Emergencies
5.1 Patients with chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding or loss of consciousness are treated as emergencies.
5.2 Emergency patients go straight to the treatment room and skip registration. Registration is completed afterwards by a family member or staff.
5.3 Outside clinic hours, emergencies go to the Emergency Department of Hospital Daerah Mawar.
